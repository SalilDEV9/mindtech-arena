HOW TO RUN IDEA FORGE

RELEASE → BID → ALLOCATE

  1. Release: open 02 Release PS and click Release this PS. All teams can then read it.
  2. Show & bid: click Show / allocate beside the PS. Choose the winning team and final credits below.
  3. Allocate: click Confirm allocation to team. This assigns the PS and deducts credits once.

For the event screen, open the projector and click Enable reveal buzzer once. Test team logins in an Incognito window so your admin login stays active.

GIVE A PS TO A TEAM
